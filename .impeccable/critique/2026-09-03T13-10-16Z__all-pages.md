## Overall Impression

Engineering judgment is well ahead of design judgment. The router's addressable-vs-transient split, per-pick exam autosave with `이어서 (n/28)`, IME-proof `e.code === 'KeyR'`, the `.screen-loading` bg-matched placeholder with `aria-busy` — all careful. Then the app tells a first-time user who just completed a lesson they have learned 0 of 6040 things and are weak at 6.

The pattern on all ten screens: mechanism right, presentation demotivating or broken. Biggest opportunity is not a redesign — it is what the app says about progress, and what it does the instant the user gets something wrong.

Two outright defects: the quiz cannot be completed in landscape or at 200% text, and `.big-audio`'s label breaks 70px out of its circle on all 8 청해 items.

## What's Working

1. **ChoiceGrid as a two-mode primitive.** `mode="answer"` / `mode="feedback"` let the SRS lesson and the JLPT exam share one component; both inherit `disabled` in feedback, `aria-pressed` only in answer mode, `aria-hidden` on ✓/✗, and a sibling `role="status"` speaking "오답. 정답은 a".
2. **Exam persistence and recovery** (`JlptExam.tsx:62-94`). Per-pick localStorage writes; `이어서 (n/28)` beside `처음부터`; tap-to-deselect; blank-submit routes to the first blank instead of warning.
3. **Focus visibility is flawless, measured.** 14 tab stops × 11 states: every focusable element got `outline: 3px solid #ffffff`, tab order matched visual order, `:focus:not(:focus-visible)` suppresses for mouse. Zero unnamed buttons, zero missing alt, zero unlabeled inputs, zero console errors/warnings, zero horizontal overflow at 320/390/1440.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Quiz unfinishable in landscape and at large text
`.lesson` / `.listen-play` are `height: 100dvh; overflow: hidden` (DESIGN.md:57-59). At 844×390 options 3–4 clip, 계속 sits at y=413 in a 390px viewport, neither container nor document scrolls. Rotation, attached keyboard, split-screen and 150–200% text zoom all hit it. Touch users are trapped with no error and no hint.
**Fix:** `min-height: 100dvh; overflow-y: auto`, retain `overscroll-behavior: none` — ~95% of the bounce benefit at none of the risk. Or gate the locked layout behind `@media (min-height: 600px) and (orientation: portrait)`.
**Command:** `/impeccable adapt`

### [P0] `.big-audio` "🔊 듣기" overflows its own circle on every JLPT listening item
`JlptQuestionView.tsx:33-41` puts the label in a 96×96 `border-radius: 50%` button at `font-size: 40px`, `overflow: visible`. Measured `scrollHeight: 166` vs `height: 96`. 8 of 28 N5 items are 청해, so the most-tapped control in the listening section is visibly broken on first sight. Works in Lesson only because its label is a bare emoji.
**Fix:** drop the text (button already has `aria-label="듣기"`), move "듣기" to the hint line; or a `.audio-pill` variant (auto width, `min-height: 56px`, `font-size: 20px`, `border-radius: var(--radius)`).
**Command:** `/impeccable polish`

### [P1] Progress reads zero, and freshly-learned letters are labeled 약한 것
`LEARNED_BOX = 3` (`srs.ts:27`) needs two correct answers after the intro; `weakItems` (`srs.ts:90`) returns everything with `box < 3`. Verified after one complete lesson: `전체 0 / 6040 익힘`, `히라가나 0 / 104`, empty bar, CTA still `시작하기`, and `🔁 약한 것만 6개`. The headline number cannot move for ~3 sessions — exactly the retention window. A fresh deck also yields six consecutive `intro` cards (`srs.ts:129-132`): six taps of 다음, no quiz, no feedback, no score, so the first session contains zero moments of the app's best mechanic.
**Fix:** two-segment `.progress-bar` — 배우는 중 (`box 1-2`, `--surface-2`) and 익힘 (`box ≥ 3`, `--primary`), both labeled. Rename the tile 🔁 복습할 것. State the delta on Complete ("히라가나 6자 새로 배움 · 전체 6/6040"). Consider quizzing immediately after a 1.5s sounded reveal instead of withholding the mechanic until session two.
**Command:** `/impeccable clarify`, then `/impeccable onboard`

### [P1] White-on-`--primary` fails WCAG AA on every primary button
`#ffffff` on `#7c5cff` = **4.35:1** against 4.5:1. At 17px/700 it is below the 18.66px large-text threshold, so no exemption. Measured: `.btn-primary` on home/week-1/exam/report/quiz/complete/listen, `.deck-tab.active`, all four `.jlpt-level-btn`, three `.seg-item.on`, `.key-hint` at 10px/700. Purple-as-text worse: `strong` #7c5cff on `--bg` = 4.33:1 (home's "전체 0 / 6040"), `.learn-week-no` on `--surface` = 3.79:1 × 24 instances, `.meaning.speaking` = 4.33:1. DESIGN.md's a11y section claims this floor is met.
**Fix:** darken `--primary` to ~`#6a45f5` (white ≈5.0:1) and add a separate `--primary-text` at ~`#a78bff` for purple-on-dark text (≈7:1 on `--bg`). Keep `--primary` for fills only. One token pair fixes 40+ measured instances.
**Command:** `/impeccable audit`

### [P1] A wrong answer teaches nothing — and the exam's ending punishes
Wrong pick → one red tile, one green, 계속. No reading, no Hangul, no mnemonic, no replay (`Quiz.tsx` gates 🔊 to `qtype === 'listen'`). 계속 appearing shifts the option grid up 37px (505→468), so the tile under the thumb moves as it is tapped. The exam ends the same way: score at `.score` (20px `--muted`) while the negative verdict is 20px/800 in full `--text`; when `inconclusive` fires the study CTA is suppressed (`JlptReport.tsx:137`) and 다시 풀기 becomes primary while `오답 다시 보기 (27)` is a ghost. No pass reference despite knowing the level. `.jlpt-hero` declares `.card` without a background, so `.card{flex:1}` opens two ~200px voids.
**Fix:** always render reading + Hangul gloss + 🔊 replay in the feedback state, and reserve the 계속 button's height so nothing reflows. On the report: score as hero with "N5 합격선은 약 50%", `오답 다시 보기` as `.btn-primary` unconditionally, keep a study CTA in the inconclusive branch, `background: var(--surface); flex: none` on `.jlpt-hero`.
**Command:** `/impeccable harden`, then `/impeccable layout`

### [P2] Active deck tab invisible on deep link; all 11 tabs under-size
`.deck-switch` (`Home.tsx:102-114`) has no `scrollIntoView`, `scrollbar-width: none`, a fade mask. On `/jp-study/deck/cloze` no tab renders as selected. Deck URLs are deliberately shareable/indexable (27 sitemap URLs), so 5 off-screen decks land users in a tab bar showing someone else's selection. Tabs measure 39px against the app's own 44px floor. Desktop: 480px column in 1440px = 66.7% of viewport empty, with every grammar deck the JLPT mode depends on behind the fade. Widget is `role="tablist"`/`role="tab"` with no `tabpanel`, no `aria-controls`, no arrow keys.
**Fix:** `scrollIntoView({ inline: 'center', block: 'nearest' })` on `deck.id`; 44px padding; `aria-controls` + arrow keys; wrap to two rows at `min-width: 520px`. Better: a 덱 button opening a grouped bottom sheet (문자 / 어휘 / 문법·회화 / 시험) fixes active state, targets, desktop and chunking at once.
**Command:** `/impeccable adapt`

## Questions to Consider

1. If the kana quiz answered in Hangul, what else could you delete — `.romaji`, the `optionText` romaji branch, the `읽기` question type? Is romaji load-bearing or inherited from English kana apps?
2. What is Home for after week one? Eleven tabs and six CTAs, or one sentence ("오늘 복습할 12개가 밀렸어요") and one button?
3. The exam knows the level, part and score. Why doesn't it know the pass line? One reference mark turns "4%" from punishment into measurement — and could gate the level list instead of offering four indistinguishable rows.
4. A 12-week curriculum, a 6,040-item drill bank and a JLPT diagnostic, with no link between any of them. Tag each week with its decks and JLPT parts and 오답 → 3주차 조사 → `빈칸/조사 に` becomes one tap. The content exists; only the wiring is missing.
5. Why does the drill screen hold the viewport still? The stated reason is overscroll bounce; the cost is a quiz that can't be completed in landscape or at large text.
